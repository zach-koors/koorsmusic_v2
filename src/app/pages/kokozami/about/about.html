drum beats and synth treats

kokozami started as a way for me to use drumming and producing as an outlet for thoughts, memories, restlessness and exploration.

the music pulls from jazz fusion, post-rock, and electronic influences, but it’s really just me chasing sounds that feel honest — drum patterns that loop me into a trance or make my heart race, bass lines that embrace or punch, melodies that sing or yell.

i’m zach koors, a drummer and producer from columbus, ohio. kokozami is where i get to explore what it all means, and hopefully make something that resonates with you too.
