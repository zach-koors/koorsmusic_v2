kokozami is a solo project of zach koors, a musician and producer based in columbus, oh.

blending elements of indie rock, dream pop, and electronic music, kokozami crafts immersive soundscapes that explore themes of nostalgia, identity, and human connection. with a penchant for lush melodies and intricate production, kokozami's music invites listeners on a journey through both introspective and expansive sonic territories.
